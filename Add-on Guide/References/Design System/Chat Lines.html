Chat Lines

Every chat line the Style Guide defines, drawn in the game's chat box in its exact colors and format. Each line carries a number; its card below names the rule it shows and the colors it uses.

How to read itClick a line in the chat box to jump to its card. "Add-on Name" stands for the Full Name and "Short Name" for the Short Name (Style Guide → ADD-ON NAMES). The player's character here is Aiek, a Paladin; everyone else comes from the house roster (OPTIONS PANEL → Example Lines). The star stands in for the add-on's target marker, which the receiving client draws as the mark.

Example Lines

On an options panel, every feature that prints or sends a line shows it under its control, drawn as it will arrive with the settings chosen (Style Guide → OPTIONS PANEL → Example Lines). The silver label reads Example with no colon; the lines sit indented under it, a blank line apart, and follow the dropdown.

Announce in
  • Print draws the exact string the print helper builds. Party, Raid and Instance all draw as party chat, from CHAT_PARTY_GET, in ChatTypeInfo.PARTY (fallback AAAAFF). A whisper draws as the person receiving it sees it, from CHAT_WHISPER_GET, in ChatTypeInfo.WHISPER (fallback FF80FF).
  • A report with a total shows the total over a detail line. Nothing prints or sends: no Example shows.
