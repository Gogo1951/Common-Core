Windows and Tooltips

An add-on's own window and the lines it adds to tooltips, drawn to the Style Guide's WINDOWS and TOOLTIPS rules.

Try itDrag the window by its title bar; it reopens where you left it. Close it with its button or Escape. Start combat with and without protected buttons and watch what changes. Turn on the spacing overlay to see the window's numbers.

Tooltip Lines

The add-on's block sits under the game's own lines: a spacer, then its header, the title gold on the left and the Short Name gray on the right. These lines stay tooltip lines; only a tooltip on the add-on's own button becomes a menu (02 UX Review → Menus, Not Tooltips). Last, a tooltip on the add-on's own widget.

  • A spacer, a header, then the rows, after every game line. The header: the title gold on the left, the Short Name gray on the right (Inventory Report … GtG), in Title Case.Style Guide → TOOLTIPS → Blocks in the Game's Tooltips
  • Rows one step in. Labels white, or the thing's own color (quality, class); values white on the right; a word beside a number gray, number first.Style Guide → TOOLTIPS; COLORS
  • States like a menu's: On green, Off gray. Red only for a requirement the player doesn't meet.Style Guide → TOOLTIPS
  • A total goes in a row at the bottom, never beside the title.Style Guide → TOOLTIPS
  • No modifier keys. The block is the same with Shift held or not.Style Guide → TOOLTIPS
  • On the add-on's own widget: a gold title over a white body, then each click hint as Action … Click, blue and gray, after one spacer.Style Guide → TOOLTIPS → Tooltips on the Add-on's Own Widgets
